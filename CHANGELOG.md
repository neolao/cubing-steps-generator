### Added

- Users can type a Rubik's Cube move sequence and get a printable A4 sheet of numbered steps, each showing the cube before the move with an arrow on the turned layer and a x2 marker for half turns, then download it as a PDF generated in the browser. Sequences longer than 30 steps continue on a new page, and invalid moves are explained next to the field.
