How it works

Cubing Steps Generator turns a Rubik's Cube move sequence into a printable PDF of numbered solving steps. Each step shows the cube before the move, with an arrow on the layer to turn. Repeated turns carry a red x2 or x3 marker.

Move notation

Write the faces U (up), D (down), L (left), R (right), F (front) and B (back), separated by spaces. A turn is clockwise by default; add an apostrophe for counter-clockwise (U'), or a 2 or 3 to repeat it (L2, L3). Example: F L F U' R U F2 L2 U'.

Questions

Is the PDF generated on a server?

No. The sheet is built in your browser and nothing is uploaded, so your sequences stay private.

How long can a sequence be?

Any length. A page holds up to 30 steps, and longer sequences continue on new A4 pages.

Can I start from a scrambled cube?

Yes. Choose the starting orientation, give a starting sequence, and add a final-state cube after the last step.
