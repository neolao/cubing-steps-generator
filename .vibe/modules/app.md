# Module: app
**Role:** The page: sequence field, options (final state, starting orientation, starting sequence), live validation per field, sheet preview, PDF download with its busy, success and failure states. Every text comes from the injected translation. It reads the sequence and options from the URL query and writes them into the language-selector links (`nav.language-selector`, generated by `modules/pages.md`) so switching language keeps what was typed.
**Files:** `src/app.ts`, `src/main.ts`, `src/styles.css`, `public/` (robots.txt, icons and the share image); the language pages themselves belong to `modules/pages.md`
**Exports:** `mountApp(root, deps)`, `AppDeps` (PDF, save, debounce, translation, URL query), `EXAMPLE_SEQUENCE`
**Depends on:** `modules/moves.md`, `modules/sheet.md`, `modules/cube.md`, `modules/pdf.md`, `modules/i18n.md`
