# Module: i18n
**Role:** One typed dictionary per language (English, French, Spanish, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean) holding every user-facing text and the text of each language page, plus the registry of languages and their page paths.
**Files:** `src/i18n/types.ts`, `src/i18n/index.ts`, `src/i18n/en.ts`, `src/i18n/fr.ts`, `src/i18n/es.ts`, `src/i18n/it.ts`, `src/i18n/zh.ts`, `src/i18n/ja.ts`, `src/i18n/ko.ts`
**Exports:** `Translation`, `FaqEntry`, `Lang`, `LANGS`, `LANGUAGES`, `translationFor(htmlLang): Translation`, one dictionary per language
**Depends on:** `modules/cube.md`
