# Module: cube
**Role:** Simulates the cube as 54 colored stickers, starts it in any of the 24 orientations and applies the 24 moves (6 faces and the middle layer M, each as a quarter turn, its reverse, a half or three-quarter turn).
**Files:** `src/cube.ts`
**Exports:** `solvedCube(orientation?)`, `Orientation`, `DEFAULT_ORIENTATION`, `COLORS`, `oppositeColor`, `isValidOrientation`, `validFrontColors`, `applyMove(state, move)`, `applySequence(state, moves)`, `stickerPosition(face, row, col)`, `FACE_ORDER`, `FACE_NORMAL`, `CubeState`, `Color`
**Depends on:** `modules/moves.md`
